# Supervisor Observation

Use synthetic or explicitly approved training records. Do not place PHI in this document.

## Observe

- The learner begins from the correct queue or workspace.
- The learner verifies identity, ownership, and source evidence before acting.
- The learner performs only actions permitted to the assigned role.
- The learner identifies at least two stop conditions without prompting.
- The learner preserves conflicting evidence rather than forcing a clean answer.
- The learner completes the correct handoff and verifies the resulting state.
- The learner recovers one conflict or failure without duplicate creation or data loss.
- The learner can locate the current job aid instead of relying only on memory.

## Outcome

- Ready for independent use
- Ready with targeted coaching
- Not yet ready; repeat assigned modules and observed scenario

Record formal workforce sign-off only in the organization's approved learning or personnel
system. Pipeline's Learning Center records preparation progress, not employment credentials.
